And it came to pass, when Jehovah would take up Elijah by a whirlwind into heaven, that Elijah went with Elisha from Gilgal.
And Elijah said unto Elisha,
Tarry here, I pray thee; for Jehovah hath sent me as far as Beth-el.
And Elisha said,
As Jehovah liveth, and as thy soul liveth, I will not leave thee.
So they went down to Beth-el.
