And the angel of Jehovah said unto Elijah,
Go down with him: be not afraid of him.
And he arose, and went down with him unto the king.
And he said unto him,
Thus saith Jehovah,
Forasmuch as thou hast sent messengers to inquire of Baal-zebub, the god of Ekron, is it because there is no God in Israel to inquire of his word? therefore thou shalt not come down from the bed whither thou art gone up, but shalt surely die.
