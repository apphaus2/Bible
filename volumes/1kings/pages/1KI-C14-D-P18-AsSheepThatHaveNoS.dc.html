And the king of Israel said,
Take Micaiah, and carry him back unto Amon the governor of the city, and to Joash the king's son; and say, Thus saith the king, Put this fellow in the prison, and feed him with bread of affliction and with water of affliction, until I come in peace.
And Micaiah said,
If thou return at all in peace, Jehovah hath not spoken by me.
And he said,
Hear, ye peoples, all of you.
