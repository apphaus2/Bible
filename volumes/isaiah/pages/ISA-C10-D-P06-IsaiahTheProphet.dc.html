So the servants of king Hezekiah came to Isaiah.
And Isaiah said unto them,
Thus shall ye say to your master, Thus saith Jehovah,
Be not afraid of the words that thou hast heard, wherewith the servants of the king of Assyria have blasphemed me.
Behold, I will put a spirit in him, and he shall hear tidings, and shall return unto his own land; and I will cause him to fall by the sword in his own land.
