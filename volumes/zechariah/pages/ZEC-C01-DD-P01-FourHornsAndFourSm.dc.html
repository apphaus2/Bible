And I lifted up mine eyes, and saw, and, behold, four horns.
And I said unto the angel that talked with me,
What are these?
And he answered me,
These are the horns which have scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem.

These are the horns which scattered Judah, so that no man did lift up his head; but these are come to terrify them, to cast down the horns of the nations, which lifted up their horn against the land of Judah to scatter it.
