And the angel that talked with me came again, and waked me, as a man that is wakened out of his sleep.
And he said unto me,
What seest thou?
And I said,
I have seen, and, behold, a candlestick all of gold, with its bowl upon the top of it, and its seven lamps thereon; there are seven pipes to each of the lamps, which are upon the top thereof;
and two olive-trees by it, one upon the right side of the bowl, and the other upon the left side thereof.
