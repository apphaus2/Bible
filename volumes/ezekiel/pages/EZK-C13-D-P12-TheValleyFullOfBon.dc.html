The hand of Jehovah was upon me, and he brought me out in the Spirit of Jehovah, and set me down in the midst of the valley; and it was full of bones. And he caused me to pass by them round about: and, behold, there were very many in the open valley; and, lo, they were very dry.

And he said unto me,
Son of man, can these bones live?
And I answered,
O Lord Jehovah, thou knowest.
Again he said unto me,
Prophesy over these bones, and say unto them, O ye dry bones, hear the word of Jehovah.
