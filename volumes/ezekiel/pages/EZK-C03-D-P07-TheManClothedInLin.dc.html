And to the others he said in my hearing,
Go ye through the city after him, and smite: let not your eye spare, neither have ye pity; slay utterly the old man, the young man and the virgin, and little children and women; but come not near any man upon whom is the mark: and begin at my sanctuary.
Then they began at the old men that were before the house. And he said unto them,
Defile the house, and fill the courts with the slain: go ye forth.
And they went forth, and smote in the city.
