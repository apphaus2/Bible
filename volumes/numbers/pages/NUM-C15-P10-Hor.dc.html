KADESHNUM 33:36
And they journeyed from Ezion-geber, and encamped in the wilderness of Zin (the same is Kadesh).
MOUNT HORNUM 33:37
And they journeyed from Kadesh, and encamped in mount Hor, in the edge of the land of Edom.
AARONNUM 33:38
And Aaron the priest went up into mount Hor at the commandment of Jehovah, and died there, in the fortieth year after the children of Israel were come out of the land of Egypt, in the fifth month, on the first day of the month. And Aaron was a hundred and twenty and three years old when he died in mount Hor.
ARADNUM 33:40
And the Canaanite, the king of Arad, who dwelt in the South in the land of Canaan, heard of the coming of the children of Israel.
ZALMONAHNUM 33:41
And they journeyed from mount Hor, and encamped in Zalmonah.
PUNONNUM 33:42
And they journeyed from Zalmonah, and encamped in Punon.
