And there were certain men, who were unclean by reason of the dead body of a man, so that they could not keep the passover on that day: and they came before Moses and before Aaron on that day:
NUM 9:6
and those men said unto him,
We are unclean by reason of the dead body of a man: wherefore are we kept back, that we may not offer the oblation of Jehovah in its appointed season among the children of Israel?
NUM 9:7
And Moses said unto them,
Stay ye, that I may hear what Jehovah will command concerning you.
NUM 9:8
