AN INSTRUMENT OF IRONNUM 35:16
But if he smote him with an instrument of iron, so that he died, he is a murderer: the murderer shall surely be put to death.
A STONE IN THE HANDNUM 35:17
And if he smote him with a stone in the hand, whereby a man may die, and he died, he is a murderer: the murderer shall surely be put to death.
A WEAPON OF WOODNUM 35:18
Or if he smote him with a weapon of wood in the hand, whereby a man may die, and he died, he is a murderer: the murderer shall surely be put to death.
THE AVENGER OF BLOODNUM 35:19
The avenger of blood shall himself put the murderer to death: when he meeteth him, he shall put him to death.
OF HATREDNUM 35:20–21
And if he thrust him of hatred, or hurled at him, lying in wait, so that he died, or in enmity smote him with his hand, so that he died; he that smote him shall surely be put to death; he is a murderer: the avenger of blood shall put the murderer to death, when he meeteth him.
