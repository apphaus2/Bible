NUM 22:1
And Balak the son of Zippor saw all that Israel had done to the Amorites. And Moab was sore afraid of the people, because they were many: and Moab was distressed because of the children of Israel.
And Moab said unto the elders of Midian,
Now will this multitude lick up all that is round about us, as the ox licketh up the grass of the field.
And Balak the son of Zippor was king of Moab at that time.
