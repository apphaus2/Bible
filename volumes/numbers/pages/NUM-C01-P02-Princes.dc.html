And these are the names of the men that shall stand with you.
NUM 1:5
REUBENNUM 1:5
Of Reuben: Elizur the son of Shedeur.
SIMEONNUM 1:6
Of Simeon: Shelumiel the son of Zurishaddai.
JUDAHNUM 1:7
Of Judah: Nahshon the son of Amminadab.
ISSACHARNUM 1:8
Of Issachar: Nethanel the son of Zuar.
ZEBULUNNUM 1:9
Of Zebulun: Eliab the son of Helon.
EPHRAIMNUM 1:10
Of the children of Joseph: Of Ephraim: Elishama the son of Ammihud.
MANASSEHNUM 1:10
Of Manasseh: Gamaliel the son of Pedahzur.
BENJAMINNUM 1:11
Of Benjamin: Abidan the son of Gideoni.
DANNUM 1:12
Of Dan: Ahiezer the son of Ammishaddai.
ASHERNUM 1:13
Of Asher: Pagiel the son of Ochran.
GADNUM 1:14
Of Gad: Eliasaph the son of Deuel.
NAPHTALINUM 1:15
Of Naphtali: Ahira the son of Enan.
These are they that were called of the congregation, the princes of the tribes of their fathers; they were the heads of the thousands of Israel.
