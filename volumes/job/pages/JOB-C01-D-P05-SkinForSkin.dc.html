Then Job arose, and rent his robe, and shaved his head, and fell down upon the ground, and worshipped;
JOB 1:20
and he said,
Naked came I out of my mother's womb, and naked shall I return thither: Jehovah gave, and Jehovah hath taken away; blessed be the name of Jehovah.
In all this Job sinned not, nor charged God foolishly.
JOB 1:21–22
Again it came to pass on the day when the sons of God came to present themselves before Jehovah, that Satan came also among them to present himself before Jehovah.
And Jehovah said unto Satan,
From whence comest thou?
And Satan answered Jehovah, and said,
From going to and fro in the earth, and from walking up and down in it.
