Then said I,
Ah, Lord Jehovah! behold, I know not how to speak; for I am a child.
But Jehovah said unto me,
Say not, I am a child; for to whomsoever I shall send thee thou shalt go, and whatsoever I shall command thee thou shalt speak.
Be not afraid because of them; for I am with thee to deliver thee, saith Jehovah.
JER 1:6–8
Then Jehovah put forth his hand, and touched my mouth; and Jehovah said unto me,
Behold, I have put my words in thy mouth:
see, I have this day set thee over the nations and over the kingdoms, to pluck up and to break down and to destroy and to overthrow, to build and to plant.
