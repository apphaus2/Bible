And the word of Jehovah came unto me the second time, saying,
What seest thou?
And I said,
I see a boiling caldron; and the face thereof is from the north.
Then Jehovah said unto me,
Out of the north evil shall break forth upon all the inhabitants of the land.
JER 1:13–14
