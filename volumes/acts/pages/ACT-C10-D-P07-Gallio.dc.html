But when Gallio was proconsul of Achaia, the Jews with one accord rose up against Paul and brought him before the judgment-seat,
saying,
This man persuadeth men to worship God contrary to the law.
ACTS 18:12–13
But when Paul was about to open his mouth, Gallio said unto the Jews,
If indeed it were a matter of wrong or of wicked villany, O ye Jews, reason would that I should bear with you:
but if they are questions about words and names and your own law, look to it yourselves; I am not minded to be a judge of these matters.
ACTS 18:14–15
