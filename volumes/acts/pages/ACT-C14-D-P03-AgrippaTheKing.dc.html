Now when certain days were passed, Agrippa the king and Bernice arrived at Cæsarea, and saluted Festus.
ACTS 25:13
And as they tarried there many days, Festus laid Paul's case before the king, saying,
There is a certain man left a prisoner by Felix;
about whom, when I was at Jerusalem, the chief priests and the elders of the Jews informed me, asking for sentence against him.
To whom I answered, that it is not the custom of the Romans to give up any man, before that the accused have the accusers face to face, and have had opportunity to make his defence concerning the matter laid against him.
