And both of them disclosed themselves unto the garrison of the Philistines: and the Philistines said,
Behold, the Hebrews come forth out of the holes where they had hid themselves.
And the men of the garrison answered Jonathan and his armorbearer, and said,
Come up to us, and we will show you a thing.
And Jonathan said unto his armorbearer,
Come up after me; for Jehovah hath delivered them into the hand of Israel.
