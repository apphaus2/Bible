And Jehovah called yet again, Samuel. And Samuel arose and went to Eli, and said,
Here am I; for thou calledst me.
And he answered,
I called not, my son; lie down again.
Now Samuel did not yet know Jehovah, neither was the word of Jehovah yet revealed unto him.
1SAM 3:6–7
And Jehovah called Samuel again the third time. And he arose and went to Eli, and said,
Here am I; for thou calledst me.
And Eli perceived that Jehovah had called the child.
1SAM 3:8
Therefore Eli said unto Samuel,
Go, lie down: and it shall be, if he call thee, that thou shalt say, Speak, Jehovah; for thy servant heareth.
So Samuel went and lay down in his place.
