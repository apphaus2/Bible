Wherefore Saul sent messengers unto Jesse, and said,
Send me David thy son, who is with the sheep.
And Jesse took an ass laden with bread, and a bottle of wine, and a kid, and sent them by David his son unto Saul. And David came to Saul, and stood before him: and he loved him greatly; and he became his armorbearer.
And Saul sent to Jesse, saying,
Let David, I pray thee, stand before me; for he hath found favor in my sight.
