And the king said unto her,
Be not afraid: for what seest thou?
And the woman said unto Saul,
I see a god coming up out of the earth.
And he said unto her,
What form is he of?
And she said,
An old man cometh up; and he is covered with a robe.
And Saul perceived that it was Samuel, and he bowed with his face to the ground, and did obeisance.
1SAM 28:13–14
