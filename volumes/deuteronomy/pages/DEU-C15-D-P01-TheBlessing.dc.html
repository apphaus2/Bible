Jehovah came from Sinai, And rose from Seir unto them; He shined forth from mount Paran, And he came from the ten thousands of holy ones: At his right hand was a fiery law for them.
Yea, he loveth the people; All his saints are in thy hand: And they sat down at thy feet; Every one shall receive of thy words.
Moses commanded us a law, An inheritance for the assembly of Jacob.
And he was king in Jeshurun, When the heads of the people were gathered, All the tribes of Israel together.
