The Jews therefore murmured concerning him, because he said, I am the bread which came down out of heaven.
And they said,
Is not this Jesus, the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know? how doth he now say, I am come down out of heaven?
JOHN 6:41–42
Jesus answered and said unto them,
Murmur not among yourselves.
No man can come to me, except the Father that sent me draw him: and I will raise him up in the last day.
JOHN 6:43–44
It is written in the prophets,
And they shall all be taught of God.
Every one that hath heard from the Father, and hath learned, cometh unto me.
Not that any man hath seen the Father, save he that is from God, he hath seen the Father.
