The woman saith unto him,
Sir, thou hast nothing to draw with, and the well is deep: whence then hast thou that living water?
Art thou greater than our father Jacob, who gave us the well, and drank thereof himself, and his sons, and his cattle?
JOHN 4:11–12
Jesus answered and said unto her,
Every one that drinketh of this water shall thirst again:
but whosoever drinketh of the water that I shall give him shall never thirst; but the water that I shall give him shall become in him a well of water springing up unto eternal life.
JOHN 4:13–14
The woman saith unto him,
Sir, give me this water, that I thirst not, neither come all the way hither to draw.
