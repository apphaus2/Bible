That disciple therefore whom Jesus loved saith unto Peter,
It is the Lord.
So when Simon Peter heard that it was the Lord, he girt his coat about him (for he was naked), and cast himself into the sea.
But the other disciples came in the little boat (for they were not far from the land, but about two hundred cubits off), dragging the net full of fishes.
JOHN 21:7–8
So when they got out upon the land, they see a fire of coals there, and fish laid thereon, and bread.
Jesus saith unto them,
Bring of the fish which ye have now taken.
Simon Peter therefore went up, and drew the net to land, full of great fishes, a hundred and fifty and three: and for all there were so many, the net was not rent.
JOHN 21:9–11
