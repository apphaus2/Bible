The Jews said unto him,
Now we know that thou hast a demon. Abraham died, and the prophets; and thou sayest, If a man keep my word, he shall never taste of death.
Art thou greater than our father Abraham, who died? and the prophets died: whom makest thou thyself?
JOHN 8:52–53
Jesus answered,
If I glorify myself, my glory is nothing: it is my Father that glorifieth me; of whom ye say, that he is your God;
and ye have not known him: but I know him; and if I should say, I know him not, I shall be like unto you, a liar: but I know him, and keep his word.
JOHN 8:54–55
Your father Abraham rejoiced to see my day; and he saw it, and was glad.
JOHN 8:56
