And as Peter was beneath in the court, there cometh one of the maids of the high priest;
and seeing Peter warming himself, she looked upon him, and saith,
Thou also wast with the Nazarene, even Jesus.
But he denied, saying,
I neither know, nor understand what thou sayest:
and he went out into the porch; and the cock crew.
MARK 14:66–68
And the maid saw him, and began again to say to them that stood by,
This is one of them.
But he again denied it. And after a little while again they that stood by said to Peter,
Of a truth thou art one of them; for thou art a Galilæan.
