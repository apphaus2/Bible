And as they went out from Jericho, a great multitude followed him.
And behold, two blind men sitting by the way side, when they heard that Jesus was passing by, cried out, saying,
Lord, have mercy on us, thou son of David.
And the multitude rebuked them, that they should hold their peace: but they cried out the more, saying,
Lord, have mercy on us, thou son of David.
MATT 20:29–31
And Jesus stood still, and called them, and said,
What will ye that I should do unto you?
They say unto him,
Lord, that our eyes may be opened.
And Jesus, being moved with compassion, touched their eyes; and straightway they received their sight, and followed him.
MATT 20:32–34
