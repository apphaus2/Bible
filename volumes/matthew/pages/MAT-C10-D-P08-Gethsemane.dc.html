Then cometh Jesus with them unto a place called Gethsemane, and saith unto his disciples,
Sit ye here, while I go yonder and pray.
And he took with him Peter and the two sons of Zebedee, and began to be sorrowful and sore troubled.
Then saith he unto them,
My soul is exceeding sorrowful, even unto death: abide ye here, and watch with me.
MATT 26:36–38
And he went forward a little, and fell on his face, and prayed, saying,
My Father, if it be possible, let this cup pass away from me: nevertheless, not as I will, but as thou wilt.
MATT 26:39
