So when Pilate saw that he prevailed nothing, but rather that a tumult was arising, he took water, and washed his hands before the multitude, saying,
I am innocent of the blood of this righteous man; see ye to it.
And all the people answered and said,
His blood be on us, and on our children.
MATT 27:24–25
