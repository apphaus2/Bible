A FALSE REPORTEX 23:1
Thou shalt not take up a false report: put not thy hand with the wicked to be an unrighteous witness.
A MULTITUDEEX 23:2–3
Thou shalt not follow a multitude to do evil; neither shalt thou speak in a cause to turn aside after a multitude to wrest justice: neither shalt thou favor a poor man in his cause.
THINE ENEMY'S OXEX 23:4–5
If thou meet thine enemy's ox or his ass going astray, thou shalt surely bring it back to him again. If thou see the ass of him that hateth thee lying under his burden, thou shalt forbear to leave him, thou shalt surely release it with him.
THE INNOCENT AND RIGHTEOUSEX 23:6–7
Thou shalt not wrest the justice due to thy poor in his cause. Keep thee far from a false matter; and the innocent and righteous slay thou not: for I will not justify the wicked.
NO BRIBEEX 23:8
And thou shalt take no bribe: for a bribe blindeth them that have sight, and perverteth the words of the righteous.
THE HEART OF A SOJOURNEREX 23:9
And a sojourner shalt thou not oppress: for ye know the heart of a sojourner, seeing ye were sojourners in the land of Egypt.
