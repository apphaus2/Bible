And they met Moses and Aaron, who stood in the way, as they came forth from Pharaoh:
EX 5:20
and they said unto them,
Jehovah look upon you, and judge: because ye have made our savor to be abhorred in the eyes of Pharaoh, and in the eyes of his servants, to put a sword in their hand to slay us.
EX 5:21
And Moses returned unto Jehovah, and said,
Lord, wherefore hast thou dealt ill with this people? why is it that thou hast sent me? For since I came to Pharaoh to speak in thy name, he hath dealt ill with this people; neither hast thou delivered thy people at all.
EX 5:22–23
And Jehovah said unto Moses,
Now shalt thou see what I will do to Pharaoh: for by a strong hand shall he let them go, and by a strong hand shall he drive them out of his land.
