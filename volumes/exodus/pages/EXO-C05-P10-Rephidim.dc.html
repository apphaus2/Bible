And all the congregation of the children of Israel journeyed from the wilderness of Sin, by their journeys, according to the commandment of Jehovah, and encamped in Rephidim: and there was no water for the people to drink.
第十七章 CHAPTER 17
EX 17:1
Wherefore the people strove with Moses, and said,
Give us water that we may drink.
And Moses said unto them,
Why strive ye with me? wherefore do ye tempt Jehovah?
EX 17:2
And the people thirsted there for water; and the people murmured against Moses, and said,
Wherefore hast thou brought us up out of Egypt, to kill us and our children and our cattle with thirst?
EX 17:3
And Moses cried unto Jehovah, saying,
What shall I do unto this people? they are almost ready to stone me.
