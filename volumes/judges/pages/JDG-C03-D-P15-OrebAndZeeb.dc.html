And Gideon sent messengers throughout all the hill-country of Ephraim, saying,
Come down against Midian, and take before them the waters, as far as Beth-barah, even the Jordan.
So all the men of Ephraim were gathered together, and took the waters as far as Beth-barah, even the Jordan.
And they took the two princes of Midian, Oreb and Zeeb; and they slew Oreb at the rock of Oreb, and Zeeb they slew at the winepress of Zeeb, and pursued Midian: and they brought the heads of Oreb and Zeeb to Gideon beyond the Jordan.
