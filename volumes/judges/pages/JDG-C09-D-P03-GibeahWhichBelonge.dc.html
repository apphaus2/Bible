But the man would not tarry that night, but he rose up and departed, and came over against Jebus (the same is Jerusalem): and there were with him a couple of asses saddled; his concubine also was with him.
When they were by Jebus, the day was far spent; and the servant said unto his master,
Come, I pray thee, and let us turn aside into this city of the Jebusites, and lodge in it.
JDG 19:10–11
And his master said unto him,
We will not turn aside into the city of a foreigner, that is not of the children of Israel; but we will pass over to Gibeah.
And he said unto his servant,
Come and let us draw near to one of these places; and we will lodge in Gibeah, or in Ramah.
JDG 19:12–13
So they passed on and went their way; and the sun went down upon them near to Gibeah, which belongeth to Benjamin. And they turned aside thither, to go in to lodge in Gibeah: and he went in, and sat him down in the street of the city; for there was no man that took them into his house to lodge.
