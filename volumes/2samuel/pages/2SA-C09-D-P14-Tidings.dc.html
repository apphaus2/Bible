And the watchman saw another man running; and the watchman called unto the porter, and said,
Behold, another man running alone.
And the king said,
He also bringeth tidings.
And the watchman said,
I think the running of the foremost is like the running of Ahimaaz the son of Zadok.
And the king said,
He is a good man, and cometh with good tidings.
2SAM 18:26–27
