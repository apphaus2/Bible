Then cried a wise woman out of the city,
Hear, hear; say, I pray you, unto Joab, Come near hither, that I may speak with thee.
And he came near unto her; and the woman said,
Art thou Joab?
And he answered,
I am.
Then she said unto him,
Hear the words of thy handmaid.
And he answered,
I do hear.
