And it came to pass after the death of Saul, when David was returned from the slaughter of the Amalekites, and David had abode two days in Ziklag; it came to pass on the third day, that, behold, a man came out of the camp from Saul, with his clothes rent, and earth upon his head: and so it was, when he came to David, that he fell to the earth, and did obeisance.
2SAM 1:1–2
第一章 CHAPTER 1
And David said unto him,
From whence comest thou?
And he said unto him,
Out of the camp of Israel am I escaped.
And David said unto him,
How went the matter? I pray thee, tell me.
And he answered,
The people are fled from the battle, and many of the people also are fallen and dead; and Saul and Jonathan his son are dead also.
