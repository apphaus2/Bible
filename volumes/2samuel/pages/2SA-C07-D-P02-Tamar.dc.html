So Amnon lay down, and feigned himself sick: and when the king was come to see him, Amnon said unto the king,
Let my sister Tamar come, I pray thee, and make me a couple of cakes in my sight, that I may eat from her hand.
Then David sent home to Tamar, saying,
Go now to thy brother Amnon's house, and dress him food.

So Tamar went to her brother Amnon's house; and he was laid down. And she took dough, and kneaded it, and made cakes in his sight, and did bake the cakes.
And she took the pan, and poured them out before him; but he refused to eat. And Amnon said,
Have out all men from me.
And they went out every man from him.
