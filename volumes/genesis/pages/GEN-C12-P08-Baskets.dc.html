When the chief baker saw that the interpretation was good, he said unto Joseph,
I also was in my dream, and, behold, three baskets of white bread were on my head: and in the uppermost basket there was of all manner of baked food for Pharaoh; and the birds did eat them out of the basket upon my head.
GEN 40:16–17
And Joseph answered and said,
This is the interpretation thereof: the three baskets are three days; within yet three days shall Pharaoh lift up thy head from off thee, and shall hang thee on a tree; and the birds shall eat thy flesh from off thee.
3 DAYS GEN 40:18–19
