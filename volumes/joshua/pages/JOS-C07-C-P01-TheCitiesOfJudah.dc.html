In the SouthJOSH 15:29–32

In the LowlandJOSH 15:33–36
In the lowland, Eshtaol, and Zorah, and Ashnah, and Zanoah, and En-gannim, Tappuah, and Enam, Jarmuth, and Adullam, Socoh, and Azekah, and Shaaraim, and Adithaim, and Gederah, and Gederothaim; fourteen cities with their villages.
In the LowlandJOSH 15:37–41
Zenan, and Hadashah, and Migdal-gad, and Dilean, and Mizpeh, and Joktheel, Lachish, and Bozkath, and Eglon, and Cabbon, and Lahmam, and Chitlish, and Gederoth, Beth-dagon, and Naamah, and Makkedah; sixteen cities with their villages.
In the LowlandJOSH 15:42–44
Libnah, and Ether, and Ashan, and Iphtah, and Ashnah, and Nezib, and Keilah, and Achzib, and Mareshah; nine cities with their villages.
In the LowlandJOSH 15:45–46
Ekron, with its towns and its villages; from Ekron even unto the sea, all that were by the side of Ashdod, with their villages.
In the LowlandJOSH 15:47
Ashdod, its towns and its villages; Gaza, its towns and its villages; unto the brook of Egypt, and the great sea, and the border thereof.
