Now it came to pass on one of those days, that he entered into a boat, himself and his disciples; and he said unto them,
Let us go over unto the other side of the lake:
and they launched forth.
But as they sailed he fell asleep: and there came down a storm of wind on the lake; and they were filling with water, and were in jeopardy.
LUKE 8:22–23
And they came to him, and awoke him, saying,
Master, master, we perish.
And he awoke, and rebuked the wind and the raging of the water: and they ceased, and there was a calm.
