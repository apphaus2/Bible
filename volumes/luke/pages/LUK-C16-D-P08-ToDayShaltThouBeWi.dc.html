And one of the malefactors that were hanged railed on him, saying,
Art not thou the Christ? save thyself and us.
But the other answered, and rebuking him said,
Dost thou not even fear God, seeing thou art in the same condemnation?
And we indeed justly; for we receive the due reward of our deeds: but this man hath done nothing amiss.
LUKE 23:39–41
And he said,
Jesus, remember me when thou comest in thy kingdom.
And he said unto him,
Verily I say unto thee, To-day shalt thou be with me in Paradise.
