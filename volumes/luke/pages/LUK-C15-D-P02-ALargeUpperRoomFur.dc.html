And the day of unleavened bread came, on which the passover must be sacrificed.
And he sent Peter and John, saying,
Go and make ready for us the passover, that we may eat.
LUKE 22:7–8
And they said unto him,
Where wilt thou that we make ready?
LUKE 22:9
And he said unto them,
Behold, when ye are entered into the city, there shall meet you a man bearing a pitcher of water; follow him into the house whereinto he goeth.
And ye shall say unto the master of the house, The Teacher saith unto thee, Where is the guest-chamber, where I shall eat the passover with my disciples?
And he will show you a large upper room furnished: there make ready.
LUKE 22:10–12
