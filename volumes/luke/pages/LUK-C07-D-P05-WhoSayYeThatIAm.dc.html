And it came to pass, as he was praying apart, the disciples were with him: and he asked them, saying,
Who do the multitudes say that I am?
LUKE 9:18
And they answering said,
John the Baptist; but others say, Elijah; and others, that one of the old prophets is risen again.
LUKE 9:19
And he said unto them,
But who say ye that I am?
LUKE 9:20
And Peter answering said,
The Christ of God.
LUKE 9:20
