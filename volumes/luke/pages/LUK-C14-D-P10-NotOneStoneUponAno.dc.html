And they asked him, saying,
Teacher, when therefore shall these things be? and what shall be the sign when these things are about to come to pass?
LUKE 21:7
And he said,
Take heed that ye be not led astray: for many shall come in my name, saying, I am he; and, The time is at hand: go ye not after them.
And when ye shall hear of wars and tumults, be not terrified: for these things must needs come to pass first; but the end is not immediately.
LUKE 21:8–9
Then said he unto them,
Nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom;
and there shall be great earthquakes, and in divers places famines and pestilences; and there shall be terrors and great signs from heaven.
