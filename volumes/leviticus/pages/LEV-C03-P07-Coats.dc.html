And Moses called Mishael and Elzaphan, the sons of Uzziel the uncle of Aaron, and said unto them,
Draw near, carry your brethren from before the sanctuary out of the camp.
LEV 10:4
So they drew near, and carried them in their coats out of the camp, as Moses had said.
LEV 10:5
And Moses said unto Aaron, and unto Eleazar and unto Ithamar, his sons,
Let not the hair of your heads go loose, neither rend your clothes; that ye die not, and that he be not wroth with all the congregation: but let your brethren, the whole house of Israel, bewail the burning which Jehovah hath kindled. And ye shall not go out from the door of the tent of meeting, lest ye die; for the anointing oil of Jehovah is upon you.
And they did according to the word of Moses.
