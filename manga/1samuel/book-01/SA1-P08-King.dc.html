Then all the elders of Israel gathered themselves together, and came to Samuel unto Ramah; and they said unto him,
Behold, thou art old, and thy sons walk not in thy ways: now make us a king to judge us like all the nations.
第八章 CHAPTER 8
But the thing displeased Samuel, when they said, Give us a king to judge us. And Samuel prayed unto Jehovah.
And Jehovah said unto Samuel,
Hearken unto the voice of the people in all that they say unto thee; for they have not rejected thee, but they have rejected me, that I should not be king over them.
But the people refused to hearken unto the voice of Samuel; and they said,
Nay: but we will have a king over us, that we also may be like all the nations, and that our king may judge us, and go out before us, and fight our battles.
