The first book of the Bible — from the first light to Joseph in Egypt, in five books.

  3. Book Three · Genesis 11–22The PromiseThe tower of Babel, the call of Abram, Sodom and Gomorrah, and the mountain of Moriah.8 pages

Exodus 出エジプト記

The second book of the Bible — the bondage in Egypt, Moses at the burning bush, the plagues, and the crossing of the Red Sea.

  1. Book One · Exodus 1–4The Bush That BurnedThe new king who knew not Joseph, the ark of bulrushes, Moses in Midian, the burning bush, I AM THAT I AM, and the rod.8 pages
  2. Book Two · Exodus 5–11The PlaguesLet my people go: the rods that became serpents, the river turned to blood, frogs, lice, flies, hail and fire, locusts, darkness, and the warning of midnight.8 pages
  3. Book Three · Exodus 12–15The Red SeaThe passover lamb, the blood on the doorposts, the great cry at midnight, the pillars of cloud and fire, the sea divided, and the song of Miriam.8 pages
