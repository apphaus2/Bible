In Our Image — Genesis 1:24–27

And God said,
Let the earth bring forth living creatures after their kind, cattle, and creeping things, and beasts of the earth after their kind: and it was so.
And God made the beasts of the earth after their kind, and the cattle after their kind, and everything that creepeth upon the ground after its kind: and God saw that it was good.
GEN 1:24–25
And God said,
Let us make man in our image, after our likeness: and let them have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the birds of the heavens, and over the cattle, and over all the earth, and over every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth.
GEN 1:26
And God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him;
male and female created he them.
GEN 1:27
