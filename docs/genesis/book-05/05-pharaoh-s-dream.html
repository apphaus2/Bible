Pharaoh's Dream — Genesis 41:1–7

Pharaoh dreamed: and, behold, he stood by the river. And, behold, there came up out of the river seven kine, well-favored and fat-fleshed. … And seven other kine came up after them, ill-favored and lean-fleshed. … And the ill-favored and lean-fleshed kine did eat up the seven well-favored and fat kine.
