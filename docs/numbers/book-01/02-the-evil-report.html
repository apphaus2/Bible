The Evil Report — Numbers 13:27–33

And they told him, and said,
We came unto the land whither thou sentest us; and surely it floweth with milk and honey; and this is the fruit of it.
Howbeit the people that dwell in the land are strong, and the cities are fortified, and very great: and moreover we saw the children of Anak there.
NUM 13:28
And Caleb stilled the people before Moses, and said,
Let us go up at once, and possess it; for we are well able to overcome it.
But the men that went up with him said,
We are not able to go up against the people; for they are stronger than we.
And there we saw the Nephilim, the sons of Anak, who come of the Nephilim: and we were in our own sight as grasshoppers, and so we were in their sight.
