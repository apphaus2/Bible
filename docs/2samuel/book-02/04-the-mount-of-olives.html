The Mount of Olives — 2 Samuel 15:23–30

And all the country wept with a loud voice, and all the people passed over: the king also himself passed over the brook Kidron, and all the people passed over, toward the way of the wilderness.
And David went up by the ascent of the mount of Olives, and wept as he went up; and he had his head covered, and went barefoot:
and all the people that were with him covered every man his head, and they went up, weeping as they went up.
