Tidings — 2 Samuel 18:24–32

Now David was sitting between the two gates: and the watchman went up to the roof of the gate unto the wall, and lifted up his eyes, and looked, and, behold, a man running alone.
And the king said,
If he be alone, there is tidings in his mouth.
And the king said unto the Cushite,
Is it well with the young man Absalom?
And the Cushite answered,
The enemies of my lord the king, and all that rise up against thee to do thee hurt, be as that young man is.
