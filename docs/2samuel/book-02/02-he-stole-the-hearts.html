He Stole the Hearts — 2 Samuel 15:2–6

And Absalom rose up early, and stood beside the way of the gate:
Of what city art thou?
Thy servant is of one of the tribes of Israel.
第十五章 CHAPTER 15
Absalom said moreover,
Oh that I were made judge in the land, that every man who hath any suit or cause might come unto me, and I would do him justice!
And on this manner did Absalom to all Israel that came to the king for judgment:
so Absalom stole the hearts of the men of Israel.
