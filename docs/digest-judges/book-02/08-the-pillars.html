The Pillars — Judges 16:22–30

Howbeit the hair of his head began to grow again after he was shaven.
And Samson called unto Jehovah, and said,
O Lord Jehovah, remember me, I pray thee, and strengthen me, I pray thee, only this once, O God, that I may be at once avenged of the Philistines for my two eyes.
And Samson took hold of the two middle pillars upon which the house rested, and leaned upon them, the one with his right hand, and the other with his left.
And Samson said,
Let me die with the Philistines.
And he bowed himself with all his might;
