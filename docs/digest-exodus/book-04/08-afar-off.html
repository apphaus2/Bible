Afar Off — Exodus 20:18–21

And all the people perceived the thunderings, and the lightnings, and the voice of the trumpet, and the mountain smoking: and when the people saw it, they trembled, and stood afar off.
And they said unto Moses,
Speak thou with us, and we will hear; but let not God speak with us, lest we die.
And Moses said unto the people,
Fear not: for God is come to prove you, and that his fear may be before you, that ye sin not.
And the people stood afar off, and Moses drew near unto the thick darkness where God was.
