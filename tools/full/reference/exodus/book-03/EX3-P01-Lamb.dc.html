And Jehovah spake unto Moses and Aaron in the land of Egypt, saying,
In the tenth day of this month they shall take to them every man a lamb, according to their fathers' houses, a lamb for a household: … Your lamb shall be without blemish, a male a year old.
第十二章 CHAPTER 12
And ye shall take a bunch of hyssop, and dip it in the blood that is in the basin, and strike the lintel and the two side-posts with the blood that is in the basin;
And they shall take of the blood, and put it on the two side-posts and on the lintel, upon the houses wherein they shall eat it.
EX 12:7
And thus shall ye eat it: with your loins girded, your shoes on your feet, and your staff in your hand; and ye shall eat it in haste: it is Jehovah's passover.
