And Absalom was riding upon his mule, and the mule went under the thick boughs of a great oak, and his head caught hold of the oak,
and he was taken up between heaven and earth;
and the mule that was under him went on.
And they took Absalom, and cast him into the great pit in the forest, and raised over him a very great heap of stones: and all Israel fled every one to his tent.
