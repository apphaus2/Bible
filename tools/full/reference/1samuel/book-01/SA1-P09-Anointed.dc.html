And he had a son, whose name was Saul, a young man and a goodly: and there was not among the children of Israel a goodlier person than he: from his shoulders and upward he was higher than any of the people.
第九章 CHAPTER 9
Then Samuel took the vial of oil, and poured it upon his head, and kissed him, and said,
Is it not that Jehovah hath anointed thee to be prince over his inheritance?
第十章 CHAPTER 10
And Samuel said to all the people,
See ye him whom Jehovah hath chosen, that there is none like him among all the people?
And all the people shouted, and said,
Long live the king.
