And Gad came that day to David, and said unto him,
Go up, rear an altar unto Jehovah in the threshing-floor of Araunah the Jebusite.
第二十四章 CHAPTER 24
And the king said unto Araunah,
Nay; but I will verily buy it of thee at a price; neither will I offer burnt-offerings unto Jehovah my God which cost me nothing.
So David bought the threshing-floor and the oxen for fifty shekels of silver.
And David built there an altar unto Jehovah, and offered burnt-offerings and peace-offerings. So Jehovah was entreated for the land, and the plague was stayed from Israel.
