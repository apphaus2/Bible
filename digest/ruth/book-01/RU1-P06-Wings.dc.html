Then said Boaz unto Ruth,
Hearest thou not, my daughter? Go not to glean in another field, neither pass from hence, but abide here fast by my maidens.
Then she fell on her face, and bowed herself to the ground, and said unto him,
Why have I found favor in thy sight, that thou shouldest take knowledge of me, seeing I am a foreigner?
Jehovah recompense thy work, and a full reward be given thee of Jehovah, the God of Israel, under whose wings thou art come to take refuge.
So she gleaned in the field until even; and she beat out that which she had gleaned, and it was about an ephah of barley.
RUTH 2:12, 17
